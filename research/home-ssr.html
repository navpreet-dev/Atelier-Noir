The Craft

Made Slowly,
On Purpose

Every Atelier Noir piece begins as a hand-cut paper pattern in our Florence workshop. We produce in runs of 300 or fewer, inspect every bolt of fabric, and design for the third decade — not the third wash.

  • Small-run production — never more than 300 pieces per style
  • GOTS organic cotton, RWS wool, stone-washed silk
  • 10-year repair guarantee on every single garment
